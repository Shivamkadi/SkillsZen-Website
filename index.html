FAQ

Questions, answered clearly

Why do I need to verify my email and mobile?

Verification helps protect your account, purchases and digital downloads. The production system will activate an account only after the correct OTP is verified.

How do digital products get delivered?

After a verified successful payment, the purchased product is attached to your account library. The production version will use protected file delivery rather than exposing raw storage links.

Can I access my purchases later?

Yes. Your purchased products are organized in your account library so you can access eligible downloads again.

What happens if I have a payment or download problem?

You can contact SkillsZen support from your account and provide the order details so the issue can be reviewed.

Will more products be added?

Yes. New digital learning resources, templates, tools and courses can be added to the store over time.
